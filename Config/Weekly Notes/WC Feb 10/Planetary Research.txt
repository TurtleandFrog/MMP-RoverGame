# Terrestrial Bodies in Our Solar System
	Generated with Claude AI

## Inner Planets
1. **Mercury** - The smallest and innermost planet, with a rocky surface covered in craters
2. **Venus** - Similar in size to Earth but with a thick, toxic atmosphere
3. **Earth** - Our home planet, with a diverse surface of oceans, continents, and a life-supporting atmosphere
4. **Mars** - The "Red Planet" with a thin atmosphere, polar ice caps, and evidence of past water flow

## Moons
5. **The Moon** (Earth's moon) - Our only natural satellite, with a cratered, airless surface
6. **Phobos** (Mars) - Irregularly shaped, likely a captured asteroid
7. **Deimos** (Mars) - Smaller than Phobos, also irregularly shaped
8. **Io** (Jupiter) - Volcanically active with sulfur-rich surface
9. **Europa** (Jupiter) - Ice-covered surface with possible subsurface ocean
10. **Ganymede** (Jupiter) - Largest moon in the solar system, with a differentiated interior
11. **Callisto** (Jupiter) - Heavily cratered surface
12. **Titan** (Saturn) - Only moon with a dense atmosphere, has liquid methane lakes
13. **Enceladus** (Saturn) - Ice-covered with active geysers at its south pole
14. **Mimas** (Saturn) - Features a large impact crater making it resemble the Death Star
15. **Tethys** (Saturn) - Icy surface with large canyons and craters
16. **Dione** (Saturn) - Icy surface with bright streaks and craters
17. **Rhea** (Saturn) - Second-largest moon of Saturn, heavily cratered
18. **Iapetus** (Saturn) - Notable for its two-toned surface
19. **Titania** (Uranus) - Largest moon of Uranus
20. **Oberon** (Uranus) - Second-largest moon of Uranus
21. **Ariel** (Uranus) - Brightest moon of Uranus
22. **Umbriel** (Uranus) - Darkest of Uranus's large moons
23. **Miranda** (Uranus) - Smallest of Uranus's five major moons
24. **Triton** (Neptune) - Largest moon of Neptune, with retrograde orbit
25. **Charon** (Pluto) - Largest moon of Pluto, in a binary system with Pluto

## Dwarf Planets
26. **Ceres** - Largest object in the asteroid belt
27. **Pluto** - Former ninth planet, now classified as a dwarf planet
28. **Eris** - Slightly smaller than Pluto, with one known moon
29. **Haumea** - Elongated shape due to rapid rotation
30. **Makemake** - Similar in size to Haumea

## Asteroids and Minor Bodies
31. **Vesta** - Second-most massive asteroid
32. **Pallas** - Third-most massive asteroid
33. **Hygiea** - Fourth-largest asteroid by volume and mass
34. **Psyche** - Largest M-type (metallic) asteroid

## Trans-Neptunian Objects- Planets that orbit the sun at a greater average distance than Neptune.
35. **Quaoar** - Classical Kuiper belt object
36. **Sedna** - One of the most distant known objects in the solar system
37. **Orcus** - Has similar orbit to Pluto

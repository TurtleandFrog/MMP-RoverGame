Did research into terrestrial bodies to find "suitable" planets for rover exploration.
This is done to provide reasoning behind my level choices which are as follows:

	Earth- Tutorial Planet, familiarity without difficulty.
	Mercury- Closest to sun, extreme temperature variations. Familiarity.
	Mars- Common rover planet,familiarity. Very arid.
	Venus- Thick toxic atmosphere. Familiarity. (Surface temps melt lead, high surface pressure)
	The Moon- Many craters. Familiarity.
	Titan- Lakes of liquid methane and ethane create an interersting environment.
	IO- Most volcanically active body in the solar system.
	Pluto- Icy surface. Familiarity.

	Stretch goals - Asteroids and Trans Neptunian.

I developed a script for to generation orbits for my level select scene.
I started by developing these as gameobjects, but using a script to generate them allows
me to change them much easier later, and also potentially allow my "levels" to move around the orbits.
